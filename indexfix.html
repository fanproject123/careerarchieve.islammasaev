The fighter behind the record

Islam Masaev is a Chechen-born mixed martial artist who represents Austria and trains in Graz. He is unbeaten as an amateur and has signed a professional contract with FNC. His game is built on wrestling, grappling and judo, with kickboxing and ground and pound.
